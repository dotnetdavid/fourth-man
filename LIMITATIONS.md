# Limitations and Safety Scope

Fourth Man is a structured reasoning protocol for examining important decisions and claims. It is not a truth engine, evidence engine, benchmark result, or approval authority.

## Evidence and independence

- Similar answers from multiple agents are not automatically independent evidence. Shared models, providers, prompts, training data, framing, and sources can produce correlated errors.
- A countercase is an analysis to inspect, not proof that the initial view is wrong.
- Agreement, confidence, model prestige, or a majority vote must not replace tests, source verification, calculations, experiments, or other suitable validation.
- The protocol has not established that it improves real-world decision quality. Any effectiveness claim requires a properly designed comparison and domain-appropriate review.

## Human authority

A designated human remains responsible for the decision. Fourth Man does not vote, approve work, advance governance gates, authorize deployment, or make legal, medical, financial, security, or operational decisions.

## Health-related use

Health-related use is limited to public, educational evidence appraisal or hypothetical discussion. It must not be used to diagnose a person, estimate an individual's risk, recommend treatment or medication changes, determine dosage, triage symptoms, or direct emergency action. Individual care decisions belong with qualified clinicians and appropriate emergency services.

Public health claims must be checked against current authoritative sources, their stated population and exclusions, and the relevant jurisdiction. This repository does not bundle clinical source excerpts or provide clinical decision support.

## Source and output handling

Treat sources first introduced by an analyst as unverified leads until they are independently retrieved and checked for scope and support. Preserve uncertainty and unresolved dissent instead of hiding it behind a consensus label.

Do not place credentials, personal data, patient information, confidential material, or private decision records into prompts or durable outputs. Review proposed inputs and outputs before sharing them.

## Release boundary

This repository contains the reusable skill and public documentation only. Private pilot cases, evaluator keys, scoring material, model assignments, runtime experiments, governance records, and generated run artifacts are intentionally excluded.
